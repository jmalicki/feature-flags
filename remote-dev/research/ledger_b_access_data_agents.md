## S4. Access to the server

### Ladder 1: access levels, lightest to heaviest (ready to reuse)

| Level | What it adds | Who documents it | Entries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Single sign-on plus a touch-required hardware key | Phishing-resistant login | Google (official); New York regulator on Twitter 2020 | 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 |
| 2. Short-lived ssh certificates from an internal CA, issued after login | No long-lived keys on laptops or in `authorized_keys` | Meta 2016; Netflix BLESS; Uber pam-ussh | 4.4, 4.5, 4.6 |
| 3. One logged bastion or access proxy as the only way in | A single place that authenticates, authorizes and logs | Meta bastion; Google Access Proxy; Cloudflare Access | 4.4, 4.7, 4.12, 4.13 |
| 4. Device certificate checked along with the user | Unmanaged machines cannot connect | Google 2014; Stripe 2012-2019 (ex-employee); Google Cloudtop | 4.9, 4.10, 4.15 |
| 5. Full BeyondCorp: inventory, computed trust tiers, authorization on every request | Access follows device health, recomputed continuously | Google only, papers 2014-2023 | 4.10 to 4.14 |

#### What none of the levels stops

- **4.22** With code execution on the workstation an attacker can wrap a command that needs a key touch, so the user is prompted twice and seldom notices. | Google (ex-security engineer mjg59) | 15 Apr 2023 | [HN 35585453](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35585453) | named individual | RAW | "user ends up being prompted twice" |

- **4.25** Uber 2022: a contractor's password was bought after malware on a personal device, and the contractor eventually accepted a pushed two-factor request. | Uber | Sep 2022 | [Security update](https://www.uber.com/newsroom/security-update/) | official | RAW | "Eventually, however, the contractor accepted one, and the attacker successfully logged in." |
- **4.26** The February 2025 Bybit theft (about $1.5 billion) began on a compromised developer machine at Safe{Wallet}. | Safe / Bybit | 26 Feb 2025 | [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/lazarus-hacked-bybit-via-a-breached-safe-wallet-developer-machine/) | press quoting Safe's statement | RAW | "a compromised Safe{Wallet} developer machine" | Safe's own review and Mandiant's findings still unread.

#### Ranked designs for web reading (the note's inference; sources per row)

| Rank | Design | Published as | What still leaks or gets in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
